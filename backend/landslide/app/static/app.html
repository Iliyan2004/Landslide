Tier 1 · Susceptibility screening

National CNN/DNN ensemble susceptibility (northern Pakistan; elsewhere NODATA, never "low"), terrain, and the physical stability ensemble for a rain scenario, plus population exposure. Instant and ML-based: a ranking of where slopes are prone, not a model of a specific event.

Tier 2 · Instant stability (rain library)

Builds a library of factor-of-safety maps for a ladder of 24 h rain totals (median parameters). Drag the slider for an instant map and failing area, no server call. Also gives the critical-rain map: how much rain each slope needs to fail.

Tier 4 · Earthquake-triggered landslides

Scenario earthquake → PGA field (Joyner-Boore 1981 from distance to a Wells-Coppersmith rupture centred on the nearest mapped fault) → Newmark displacement (Jibson 2007) → coseismic failures → the same runout engine. No site amplification; screening only.

Nature-based solutions

Scenario levers that act on slope stability in the whole area. The effect sizes are yours to set; nothing here is a measured performance. Uses the storm and ground settings of Tier 3 (load a rainfall series first).

Barriers (check dams)

Draw lines across the flow path with the line tool (top-left of the map, or the button). A barrier's height is measured above the ground just upslope of it. A fast flow can overtop a low barrier; the solver decides.

Auto-place barriers (expensive: every try is a full simulation)

With a zero wall cost the optimiser will choose the biggest walls. Enter a cost to make the trade-off meaningful.

Average annual loss

Runs design storms of several return periods (from the observed IDF, Tier 3 ground settings), takes the direct loss of each, and integrates loss over annual probability. It assumes a T-year storm gives the T-year loss: an order-of-magnitude figure. Each return period is a full simulation.
