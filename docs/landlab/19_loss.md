# Estimated Direct Loss

Direct loss is, for each building, expected damage ratio x footprint area x 35,000 PKR per square metre x 1 floor, summed. This is an order-of-magnitude figure for comparing scenarios: the unit cost, the single floor and the use of footprint area are assumptions, and indirect losses (road closure days, business interruption, crops, lives) are **not** estimated. The Gilgit reference run (Ch. {{ch:tier3_pipeline}}.4) gives a direct loss of about PKR 2.4 billion for 14,400 buildings in the area, with 2,469 buildings damaged at DS1 or above and 648 at DS3 or above.

> Average annual loss, which needs a defensible event-frequency model, is planned (Ch. {{ch:executive_summary}}.2). Rainfall return period would be the frequency proxy, and that assumption (storm frequency equals landslide frequency) will be stated where it is used.
